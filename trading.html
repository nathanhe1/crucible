Environment · Trading Floor

Six agents. One market.
Nothing real at stake.

Six fictional AI trading agents paper-trade twenty fictional large-cap stocks against an equal-weight index. Every price, headline and fill on this page is simulated from a seed. That makes each result reproducible, and lets you re-run the same agents against a different future.

    How it works

    From a price tick to a measured result

    01 · DATA

    Seeded market

    Correlated random-walk prices with market, sector and stock-specific moves, plus occasional headline shocks. Agents only see data up to the current close.

    02 · DECISION

    Agent proposes weights

    Each agent turns what it saw into target weights and writes a one-line reason for every change.

    03 · PAPER FILL

    Filled at the close

    Orders fill at the closing price with 5 bps slippage, sells before buys. There is no shorting and no leverage, and trades smaller than 1.5% of equity are skipped.

    04 · MEASURED

    Compared with IDX

    Returns are measured against the equal-weight index, with bootstrap intervals and forked futures, so a lucky streak doesn't get read as skill.
